Show to Staff: Skincare Shopping Korean Flashcards

Show a phrase to staff. Skin concerns are personal; for medical symptoms consult a clinician.

지성 피부예요.I have oily skin.
민감성 피부예요.I have sensitive skin.
향이 없는 제품을 찾고 있어요.I'm looking for a fragrance-free product.
이 제품 성분을 확인하고 싶어요.I'd like to check this product's ingredients.
선크림을 추천해 주세요.Could you recommend a sunscreen?
