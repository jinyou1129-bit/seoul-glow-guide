K-Pop Idol Skincare & Makeup: What Korean Celebs Actually Buy at Olive Young

From candy-gloss lips to a camera-ready base: build a Seoul shopping list inspired by your favorite K-pop looks, with real Korean phrases to show the staff.

What’s verified? Idol beauty kits change and brand campaigns are not proof of a daily routine. The products below are shopping ideas inspired by the looks; where a particular use is reported, we say so and link the source. We cannot independently verify that every idol or Cheongdam artist uses these exact products. Prices are estimates, not live quotes; stock and shades vary.

1. Jang Won-young–inspired “tanghulu” glow lips

The look is a soft color under a reflective, candy-like shine—not necessarily one magic tube. Start with comfortable, smooth lips; blot balm, dab tint on the center, blend to the edges and add a thin gloss layer if you want more shine.

Rom&nd Juicy Lasting Tint · 롬앤 쥬시 래스팅 틴트

A fruit-gloss tint with a stain: try a rosy or muted shade and swatch in daylight. Beauty retailer YesStyle reports an association between Bare Grape and Won-young; this is a retailer claim, not confirmation of her present routine. Indicative tint budget: ~₩11,000–₩16,000, depending on product and promotion.

Amuse Jel-Fit Tint · 어뮤즈 젤핏 틴트

For a jelly-like finish, use a sheer first coat, let it settle, then tap on another. A secondary account citing makeup artist Seo Ok reports shade 06 Seoul Girl was chosen for an Amuse shoot; campaign use should not be confused with an everyday favorite. Compare swatches rather than assuming the same shade will suit everyone.

2. Blackpink-inspired glass-skin prep

Think hydrated skin and a thin, even complexion—not a claim that Blackpink uses these specific items. Begin with gentle cleansing, moisturize, then apply broad-spectrum SPF as directed. Give layers time to settle before makeup.

Torriden Dive-In Serum · 토리든 다이브인 세럼

A lightweight hydrating serum for those who enjoy a watery texture. Apply a few drops before moisturizer; check ingredients and patch-test, especially if your skin is sensitive. More layers are not automatically better.

Hera Black Cushion · 헤라 블랙 쿠션

A cushion base is convenient for controlled touch-ups. Pick your shade in natural light and tap on a thin layer from the center of the face outward. Availability at individual Olive Young stores is not guaranteed; check at the counter. Cushion SPF does not replace a properly applied sunscreen layer.

3. BTS Jungkook & V–inspired everyday essentials

For a low-fuss routine inspired by fresh, comfortable-looking skin, focus on a cleanser, moisturizer, SPF and lip balm rather than copying a rumored celebrity routine. We have not verified Jungkook or V’s personal use of the products below.

Dr.G Red Blemish Clear Soothing Cream · 닥터지 레드 블레미쉬 클리어 수딩 크림

A soothing-style moisturizer to consider when you prefer a lighter texture. Check the formula for your sensitivities, patch-test first and stop if irritation occurs; it is not a treatment for persistent skin conditions.

Lip balms · 립밤

Choose a fragrance-free balm if your lips are reactive. Apply a thin layer as needed, and look for an SPF lip product during the day. Test disposable applicators rather than sharing an open tester directly.

4. Makeup-artist-inspired secret weapons

Piccasso Makeup Spatula · 피카소 메이크업 스파츌라

Use a clean metal spatula to spread a very thin layer of foundation, then tap it in with a clean sponge. Indicative price: ~₩19,000; verify the shelf price. Clean before and after use and avoid broken skin. This technique is pro-inspired, not a verified claim about every Cheongdam artist.

Mediheal Teatree Toner Pads · 메디힐 티트리 토너패드

Convenient pre-soaked pads for a quick prep step: press gently rather than scrubbing, follow the pack directions, and check the ingredients. Skip if sensitive to a listed ingredient; don’t leave pads on longer than directed.

Frequently asked questions

Do K-pop idols really use these exact products?

Some products are associated with particular looks or reported by beauty publications, but a viral claim is not proof of an idol’s routine. We identify reported use separately from inspiration.

How much do glossy Korean lip tints cost?

Rom&nd and Amuse tints are commonly around 11,000–16,000 KRW as a rough guide. Prices, sizes and promotions vary; check the shelf.

Where can I shop at Olive Young in Seoul?

Try Myeongdong Global at 53 Myeongdong-gil or Gangnam Town at 429 Gangnam-daero. Check store hours, stock and tax-refund eligibility before visiting.

How do I ask for an idol lip tint in Korean?

Show staff: 장원영 립틴트 어디 있어요? (Where is the Jang Won-young lip tint?). A name does not guarantee a product is used by that idol.
