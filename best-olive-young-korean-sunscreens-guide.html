Why K-sunscreens went viral

On TikTok and beyond, shoppers have praised many Korean sunscreens for lightweight, serum-like textures, a moisturized finish and little visible white cast. Beauty of Joseon’s Relief Sun became a social-media favorite, while SKIN1004 and Round Lab also gained international attention. A dewy “glass skin” finish is cosmetic—not a measure of UV protection. Cast and texture depend on the formula and your skin tone; try a tester.

Some formulas sold in Korea use different UV filters from versions sold in other countries. Check the actual product label rather than assuming the formulas are identical.

Chemical vs. mineral: what do the Korean labels mean?

유기자차

Yugi jacha · chemical / organic filters

Organic filters absorb UV. Many formulas feel light and may leave less visible white cast, but each formula differs.

무기자차

Mugi jacha · mineral / physical filters

Zinc oxide or titanium dioxide absorb UV and also reflect some. They are often recommended for sensitive skin, but may leave a cast. Hybrid formulas use both types.

Wear it well

Choose broad-spectrum UVA/UVB protection with SPF 30 or higher. Apply generously before sun exposure and reapply at least every two hours outdoors; follow label directions after swimming, sweating or toweling. Choose water resistance for swimming or sweating. Add shade, a hat and protective clothing. Patch-test new products and stop if irritation occurs.

Frequently asked questions

Do Korean sunscreens leave a white cast?

Many lightweight chemical-filter formulas are designed to leave little visible cast, but results differ by formula and skin tone. Mineral products can leave a white cast; try a tester.

What is the difference between 유기자차 and 무기자차?

유기자차 means chemical or organic UV filters; 무기자차 means mineral or inorganic filters, such as zinc oxide or titanium dioxide. Hybrid formulas combine both.

How often should I reapply sunscreen?

When outdoors, reapply at least every two hours and follow the label after swimming, sweating or toweling. Use a water-resistant sunscreen when swimming or sweating.
