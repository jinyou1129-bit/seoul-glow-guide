THE SEOUL SERUM EDIT

Best Korean Serums & Ampoules at Olive Young: 7 Holy-Grail Drops for Glass Skin

From hydration to a smoother-looking glow, these seven popular K-beauty formulas are worth looking for on your Olive Young trip. Stock and formulas vary by store and date; check the exact label before buying.

7 serums and ampoules to look for

1

#1 deep hydration hero

Torriden DIVE-IN Low Molecular Hyaluronic Acid Serum

Key ingredients: Hyaluronic acid, panthenol and allantoin.

Best for: Dehydrated or tight-feeling skin.

Why travelers may like it: The lightweight, water-like texture layers easily under moisturizer and makeup: a practical hydration pick after travel.

2

Calming and moisture

Anua Heartleaf 80% Moisture Soothing Ampoule

Key ingredients: Heartleaf water, glycerin, panthenol and sodium hyaluronate.

Best for: Redness-prone or acne-prone skin that feels dry.

Why travelers may like it: A soothing, non-oily feel makes this a useful low-fuss travel option; it is not an acne treatment.

3

Fermented glow

Numbuzin No.3 Skin Softening Serum

Key ingredients: Bifida ferment lysate and galactomyces ferment filtrate.

Best for: Rough-looking texture and dullness.

Why travelers may like it: The fermented formula appeals to shoppers wanting a softer-feeling, smoother-looking finish. Check the label if avoiding animal-derived ingredients.

4

Cult-classic comfort

COSRX Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence

Key ingredients: Snail secretion filtrate.

Best for: Dryness and a stressed-feeling moisture barrier.

Why travelers may like it: A popular essence that helps skin feel hydrated and look dewy; follow with moisturizer. Not vegan: avoid if sensitive to snail ingredients.

5

Brightening care

Goodal Green Tangerine Vita C Dark Spot Serum

Key ingredients: Green tangerine extract, vitamin C derivatives and niacinamide.

Best for: Uneven-looking tone and post-acne marks.

Why travelers may like it: A targeted brightening option for a glow-focused routine. Results vary; check the current label for fragrance or essential oils if sensitive.

6

Centella soothing relief

SKIN1004 Madagascar Centella Ampoule

Key ingredients: Centella asiatica extract.

Best for: Skin that feels sensitive or dry.

Why travelers may like it: A centella-focused ampoule for a simple soothing step. Formulas differ by market and version: the brand’s current US listing contains additional ingredients, so do not assume every bottle is 100% extract.

7

Pore-focused exfoliation

Medicube Zero Pore One Day Serum

Key ingredients: AHA, BHA and PHA; some versions also include LHA.

Best for: Visible pores, excess oil and rough-looking texture.

Why travelers may like it: An exfoliating option for travelers seeking a smoother look. It cannot permanently tighten pores. Formulas vary, so check the specific bottle; start slowly and avoid irritated skin.

Build a simple routine

Morning: Gentle cleanse → Torriden or a calming ampoule → moisturizer → broad-spectrum SPF 30 or higher. For uneven-looking tone, try Goodal instead of adding several new actives at once.

Evening: Cleanse → COSRX, Anua or SKIN1004 for comfort, or Numbuzin for texture → moisturizer. One serum is enough; layer from thinner to thicker only if your skin tolerates it.

Exfoliation night: Introduce Medicube on a separate evening, initially once or twice weekly as tolerated and according to the label; follow with moisturizer. Avoid combining with retinoids, other exfoliating acids or vitamin C in the same routine while starting. Wear sunscreen the next day.

Patch-test each new product and stop if it stings or causes irritation. For persistent acne, severe redness or a damaged skin barrier, consult a dermatologist. These products are cosmetics, not medical treatment.

Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between a serum and an ampoule?

Both are concentrated leave-on skincare products. Ampoules are often marketed as targeted treatments, but the names are not regulated categories; choose by formula and your skin needs.

Which serum should I try for dry skin?

Torriden DIVE-IN uses hyaluronic acid to hydrate; seal it with moisturizer. Patch-test first, especially if your skin is sensitive.

Can I layer vitamin C and exfoliating acids?

For a gentler start, use your vitamin C product in the morning and exfoliating acids on a separate evening. Follow product directions, moisturize and wear sunscreen.

Will an exfoliating serum permanently shrink pores?

No. Exfoliation can improve the appearance of clogged-looking pores and uneven texture, but pores do not permanently close. Stop if irritation occurs.

Are all seven available at every Olive Young?

Stock, sizes, packaging and formulas vary by branch and date. Ask staff to check the exact item and its current label before buying.

Can I carry serums onto a plane?

Airport and airline liquid rules vary. Check current carry-on limits and packaging requirements for your departure airport; place larger containers in checked baggage if required.
