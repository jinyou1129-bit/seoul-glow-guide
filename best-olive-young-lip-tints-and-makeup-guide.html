THE OLIVE YOUNG MAKEUP EDIT

Best Korean Lip Tints & Makeup at Olive Young: 7 Cult-Favorite K-Beauty Essentials

From candy-glazed lips to a softly perfected complexion, these seven makeup picks make a Seoul beauty haul easy to navigate.

An editorial shortlist, not a verified sales ranking. Stock, shade names, packaging and formulations vary by branch and date. No prices are quoted. Check the label, swatch hygienically and stop use if irritation occurs.

Why tourists love Tanghulu lips and the natural idol complexion

Inspired by glossy candied fruit, the Tanghulu lip look layers translucent color and glassy shine for a juicy, dimensional finish. K-beauty’s soft idol-inspired complexion pairs that shine with a thin, even base, subtle contour and diffused eye color. The appeal for travelers: portable formulas, playful shade choices and techniques you can recreate at home.

Try a thin layer of tint, let it settle, and add balm for more shine. For the base, tap on cushion foundation sparingly and build only where you want extra coverage.

7 makeup essentials to look for at Olive Young

01

Rom&nd Juicy Lasting Tint

Glassy fruit tint: A juicy, glossy color that leaves a tint as the shine fades. Sheer layers make it easy to go from a soft wash to a richer fruit lip; look for peach, rose or berry families.

Why travelers love it: For a souvenir that creates the candy-glazed lip look in one swipe.

02

Rom&nd Glasting Melting Balm

High-shine tinted balm: A melting, balmy texture with a reflective finish and a comfortable, nourishing feel. Choose a nude, rosy or mauve tone depending on your look.

Why travelers love it: For a glossy finish without the feel of a liquid tint; reapply as needed.

03

Peripera Ink Mood Glowy Tint

Lightweight watery glow: A lightweight, water-glow tint that gives a translucent shine and a longer-wearing color stain. Try coral or rose tones; the gloss may fade before the tint.

Why travelers love it: For a quick, fresh-looking lip through a day of sightseeing.

04

Clio Kill Cover The New Founwear Cushion

Semi-matte idol base: A coverage-focused cushion with a smooth semi-matte finish. Apply a thin layer with the puff and build only where needed for a polished, natural-looking base.

Why travelers love it: For a compact, portable base and targeted coverage; shade-match before buying.

05

Hera Black Cushion

Luxury semi-matte coverage: A premium cushion with a smooth, semi-matte finish and high coverage. A light layer can even the complexion; build coverage where you want it.

Why travelers love it: For a refined, camera-ready base in a travel-friendly compact.

06

Too Cool For School Artclass By Rodin Shading

Three-color contour classic: Three blendable contour tones can be mixed for subtle dimension. Classic leans warmer; Modern is cooler, while Neutral sits in between.

Why travelers love it: For soft, adjustable definition around the face or nose rather than an obvious stripe.

07

Wakemake Soft Blurring Eye Palette

Everyday 16-shade palette: A 16-shade color story combining soft mattes and shimmer or glitter textures; different palettes offer different color families.

Why travelers love it: For mixing understated daytime eyes with a little sparkle in one palette.

Warm-tone vs cool-tone shade cheat sheet

Warm-leaning

Lips: peach, coral, warm rose or soft terracotta. Try Rom&nd Pomelo Skin or a coral balm as a starting point.

Eyes & contour: peachy beige, golden brown and warmer contour tones such as Rodin Classic.

Cool-leaning

Lips: rosy pink, mauve, plum or berry. Try Rom&nd Bare Grape or a mauve balm as a starting point.

Eyes & contour: muted rose, taupe and cooler, gray-brown contour tones such as Rodin Modern.

These are starting points, not rules: personal preference and depth matter too. Shade numbers can differ between older and newer product lines or markets, so check the name and swatch. For cushions, match your jaw to your neck in daylight; ranges may be limited for deeper skin tones.

Frequently asked questions

What are Tanghulu lips?

Tanghulu lips describe a glossy, candy-coated lip look inspired by shiny candied fruit. A translucent tint or balm can create the effect; the look is a styling trend, not a product category.

How do I choose between warm and cool shades?

Start with peach, coral or warm brown for a warm-leaning look, or rose, mauve and berry for a cool-leaning look. Neutral shades can suit either. Swatch in daylight and choose what you enjoy.

Do Korean cushion shades suit every skin tone?

Not always. In-store shade ranges can be limited, especially for deeper skin tones. Swatch along your jaw in daylight, allow it to settle and ask staff about additional shades before buying.

Are these products available at every Olive Young?

No. Stock, shade names, packaging and formulas vary by branch and date. Ask staff to check the exact item or a nearby store.

Are lip tints and balms suitable for carry-on luggage?

Liquid, gel and balm rules vary by airport and airline. Check your departure airport’s current carry-on rules and pack accordingly.
