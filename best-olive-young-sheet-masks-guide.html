How to buy multi-packs for souvenirs

  1. Look for boxes or bundles. Ask for a 10-sheet set (10장 세트); not every mask is sold in that size at every branch. Other bundles may be available.
  2. Compare the label. Check the number of individual sheets, exact mask name, expiration date and any promotion terms. A gift set may contain mixed varieties.
  3. Buy one before stocking up. If you have time, patch-test and try a single sheet first. Keep gift purchases sealed and pack them flat.
  4. Plan your flight. Sachets contain essence, so check your airport and airline liquids rules before putting them in carry-on luggage.

In-store tax-refund tips

  1. Look for a participating tax-free or tax-refund store and ask staff before paying whether immediate refund is available.
  2. Bring your physical passport for eligibility verification. Ask about the current minimum, transaction and trip limits; rules and participation can change.
  3. If immediate refund is unavailable, ask for the appropriate refund paperwork and follow the provider’s departure instructions. Keep the receipt and eligible goods unopened and unused for export; customs may ask to inspect them.

For official eligibility and departure guidance, see Korea Tourism Organization’s tax-refund guide.

How to use a sheet mask safely

Patch-test a new formula, then apply to clean skin for the time on its package. Remove, pat in remaining essence and moisturize if needed. Do not use on broken or irritated skin; stop and rinse if burning or irritation occurs. A tingling sensation is not proof a product works. For daytime, finish with sunscreen.

Frequently asked questions

Which sheet mask should I choose for dry skin?

Try a hydration-focused formula such as Torriden DIVE-IN or Round Lab Birch Juice. Check the ingredient list and patch-test, especially if your skin is sensitive.

Can I buy 10-sheet boxes at Olive Young?

Some masks are sold in multipacks, including 10-sheet sets. Stock and pack sizes vary by branch and date; ask staff to check the exact box and offer.

Can tourists get an immediate tax refund at Olive Young?

Eligible visitors may be able to receive an immediate refund at participating stores. Bring your passport, ask before paying and confirm the current minimum, limits and eligibility. Keep qualifying goods unused for export.

How do I use a sheet mask?

Apply to clean skin, leave on for the time printed on the package, remove and pat in the essence. Finish with moisturizer if desired. Stop if irritation occurs.

Can I pack sheet masks in my carry-on?

Sheet masks contain liquid essence and may be subject to airport security liquids rules. Check the departure airport and airline before packing; use checked luggage if required.
