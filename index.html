Olive Young Shopping FAQ Accordion

Can I get immediate tax refund at the counter?

Yes, eligible visitors can request an immediate refund at participating counters. Bring your physical passport and spend at least ₩15,000 per receipt; other limits and eligibility rules apply.

Can I bring toner/serum in airplane carry-on?

Liquids in containers over 100 ml generally must go in checked luggage. Containers of 100 ml or less must fit the airport’s clear liquids bag; check airline and airport rules, including exceptions.

How do I read Korean cosmetic expiration dates?

Look for 제조 (manufactured) and 까지 (use by/expiry). Check the full label and any period-after-opening symbol as well.

Can tourists get a tax refund at Olive Young?

Eligible visitors may be able to claim a refund on qualifying purchases at participating stores. Bring your passport and confirm current rules and minimums with the store and Korean customs.

How do I ask for skincare help in Korea?

Show store staff a Korean flashcard describing your skin type and the product you need.

Is this site affiliated with Olive Young?

No. Seoul Glow Guide is an independent travel resource. Store policies, prices and product availability change, so confirm details with the store.
