About Seoul Glow Guide

Seoul Glow Guide is an independent, plain-English resource for visitors who want to shop skincare and K-beauty in Seoul without guessing.

What this site covers

We publish practical checklists rather than hype: how Olive Young shopping works for a visitor, how tax refunds are requested, what a shelf label actually tells you, and Korean phrases you can show to store staff. Product pages use indicative prices for orientation only — they are not live quotes, and stock, sizes, promotions and formulations change by branch.

How we write

  • We do not claim firsthand medical or dermatological advice. Skin concerns are personal; ask a clinician about persistent irritation.
  • Shortlists are editorial starting points, not verified sales rankings.
  • When a rule can change — tax refund minimums, customs checks, store participation — we say so and point to the official source.

How this site is funded

Seoul Glow Guide is free to read. Running costs may be covered by display advertising and, where marked, affiliate links: if you buy through one of those links, the site may earn a commission at no extra cost to you. Sponsored or paid placements, if we ever publish them, will be labelled.

Independence

This site is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by Olive Young or any brand mentioned. Brand and product names belong to their owners and are used to help you find items in store.

Corrections
