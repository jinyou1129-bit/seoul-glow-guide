Olive Young Tax Refund & Shopping Formula

Rules, eligibility, minimum purchase amounts and store participation can change. Confirm current requirements at the store and with Korean customs before relying on a refund.

Before you shop

  1. Bring your passport and ask whether this branch offers tax-free shopping and whether you qualify.
  2. Compare the shelf label, promotion terms, product size and final checkout price. Stock varies by location.
  3. Ask whether the refund is immediate or requires a separate claim, and keep your receipts and paperwork.
  4. Before departure, follow the instructions on the receipt and at the airport; allow extra time for any required customs validation. Do not assume every purchase qualifies.

A simple shopping formula

Choose your skin concern → pick one product per step → check the ingredient list and patch-test → compare prices and sizes → ask staff about tax refund eligibility before paying.

Questions to show staff

택스 리펀 되나요? — Is a tax refund available?
여권이 필요한가요? — Do I need my passport?
얼마 이상 구매해야 하나요? — Is there a minimum purchase?

For official guidance see Visit Korea and Korea Customs Service. Check store details at Olive Young Global.
