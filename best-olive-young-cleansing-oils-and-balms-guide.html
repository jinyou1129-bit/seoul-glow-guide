Why tourists love K-beauty double cleansing

After a day of sunscreen, city exploring and long-wear makeup, a first cleanse with an oil or balm helps dissolve makeup, sunscreen and excess sebum. Add water to turn the oil milky, rinse, then use a gentle water-based cleanser to wash away remaining residue. The satisfying melt-and-rinse ritual is an easy way to experience K-beauty at home.

Oil cleansing can loosen surface buildup around pores, but it does not permanently melt away blackheads or cure acne. Avoid scrubbing and adjust the routine if your skin feels tight or irritated.

How to double cleanse: 3 easy steps

  1. Massage on dry skin. With dry hands, gently massage an oil or balm over your dry face to loosen sunscreen and makeup. Follow the product’s eye-area directions.
  2. Emulsify and rinse. Add a little lukewarm water, massage until milky, then rinse thoroughly. For cleansing water, follow its label instead: saturate a cotton pad and wipe gently; do not treat it as an oil.
  3. Use a gentle water-based cleanser. Cleanse briefly, rinse and pat dry. Follow with moisturizer. If skin feels stripped, reduce frequency or use a single gentle cleanser.

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to double cleanse every day?

Not necessarily. An oil or balm followed by a gentle water-based cleanser can help remove makeup or sunscreen. If skin feels dry or irritated, cleanse less often or use one gentle cleanser.

Can cleansing oils remove blackheads?

They can help loosen surface oil and buildup, but they do not permanently remove blackheads or treat acne. Avoid harsh rubbing; persistent acne may need professional advice.

Which pick is good for waterproof makeup?

Banila Co Clean It Zero Original and Heimish All Clean Balm are designed to dissolve makeup, including waterproof formulas. Follow package directions and avoid getting product in your eyes.

Is Torriden DIVE-IN Cleansing Water an oil cleanser?

No. It is a cleansing water, included as a hydration-focused alternative. Apply with a cotton pad according to the label, and follow with a gentle cleanser if needed.

Can I bring cleansing oil on a plane?

Airport liquid rules vary. Containers over 100 ml generally belong in checked luggage; check your departure airport and airline rules, including how balms are classified.

Are these all available in every Olive Young store?

No. Stock, packaging and formulations vary by branch and date. Ask staff to check the exact product before making a special trip.
