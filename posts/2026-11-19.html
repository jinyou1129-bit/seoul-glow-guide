In store

Compare ingredients, size and price.

  1. Set a budget and compare price per unit.
  2. Read the exact product label, ingredients and instructions. Ask staff if unsure.
  3. Patch-test new products and stop using them if irritation occurs.
  4. Bring your passport and ask staff about current tax-refund eligibility before paying.
